Card 5.1 Quality Check

Foundation Upgrades: Real Change vs. Rewording

Every upgrade must change a decision, a number, or a business risk. If it only rewrites the owner’s copy, it fails.

Snaxxville Contract Term: Extended from 2 years to 5 years.

At 2 years, a slower store would barely recover its CapEx before renewal fees hit.

Changes Decision
K-Street Payback Disclosure: Stated payback restated from 9 mos to 27 mos at Target.

Counted from contract signing against total cash of ₱3.03M rather than the ₱799k package.

Changes Number
Marketing Claim Dropped: Removed '90% franchising success rate' claim.

The company's own historical record of 30 closures contradicted the cited statistic.
