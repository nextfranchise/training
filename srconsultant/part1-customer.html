Card 1.1: Power Customer

Customer Voice Benchmark
What's Missing: Demographics without behavior. A crew member cannot point this person out on a weekday afternoon.

"Our target market is students and young professionals aged 18 to 35, middle income, who love affordable snacks and modern store ambiance."

Why It Passes: First-person customer voice, Taglish kept, exact daily rhythm and merienda spending behavior.

"I'm a college student at the local state university campus, or a young worker earning a little above ₱440 a day, in a town with no mall. You'll recognise me because I come after class or after work in the afternoon, usually with two or three friends..."

Key Takeaways for Module 5

  • 1. The Blueprint in One Sentence: Written last, placed first. Must balance market gap, proof metric, and franchisee gain.
  • 2. Substitutes are Behaviors: Customers substitute with habits (carinderia, street cart, skipping merienda), not just competing franchise logos.
  • 3. Demand Score Threshold: A demand score below 7/10 means the territory model is invalid.
