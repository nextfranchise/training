First Principles Rigor

The 4 Evidence Status Stamps

Every single claim, number, and belief in Next Franchise must bear one of these four stamps:

Confirmed

Directly verified from POS data, government statistics (PSA), or primary receipts.

Client-Said

What the owner claims about margins, foot traffic, or ticket size. Stays yellow until verified.

Inferred

An assumption drafted by AI or modeled from industry averages. Must be validated.

Heard

An informal crew comment or customer remark heard during a branch visit.
