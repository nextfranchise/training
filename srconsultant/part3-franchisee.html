Card 3.2 Framework

The 4-Box Outcome Canvas

When asked what they want, buyers list features. The 4-box canvas uncovers what they want to happen in their actual life.

Box 1

Direct Needs

A turnkey system that operates without guesswork.

Box 2 · Key Driver

Psycho-Spiritual

Pride, dignity, and independence. Ending overseas separation.

Box 3

Social Dimension

Reputation in the town or parish as a stable entrepreneur.

Box 4

Financial Target

Capital recovery timeline grounded in the workbook.

In Sum Synthesis: Wants to stay home, earn a dependable living, and be their own boss. Wants to avoid: losing savings that took eight hard years abroad to accumulate. That fear is the real emotional hook of the pitch.
Card 3.4 Rigor

Pain DNA: Causal Chain vs. Friction List

Pains are never disconnected bullet points. They are recurring causal sequences that destroy capital.

The 4-Link Anatomy of Failure

Link 1 What Happens
Working capital is underestimated
→
Link 2 What it Causes
Staff shifts are cut, service slips
→
Link 3 Why it Repeats
Regulars drift away; volume drops
→
Link 4 What is Lost
Unit defaults; capital gone
Card 3.5 Solution DNA Match: The system does not fix this with "marketing advice." It fixes Link 1 by embedding a mandatory 3-month fixed-cost revolving fund into Mode 1 CapEx before opening.
Card 3.7 Qualification

The Readiness Scorecard Simulator

Score prospects based purely on verified actions, not enthusiasm or verbal promises. Score below 7 = Do Not Sign.

Prospect Action Verification

1. Proof of unencumbered funds shown (2.5 × package)
2. Viable commercial site identified with lease terms
3. Visited operating flagship and shadowed rush hour
4. Committed to personal full-time owner involvement
5. Paid ₱15,000 town reservation down payment
Readiness Score
0 / 10
NOT YET (DO NOT SIGN)

Threshold is 7 out of 10. Turning away unqualified buyers protects the brand and protects family savings.

Key Takeaways for Module 7

  • 1. The Absentee Investor Trap: The investor looking for "pure passive income" is the profile most likely to fail in early-stage franchising.
  • 2. Solution DNA Must Match Pain DNA: Every inclusion in the package must explicitly answer a failure mode documented in Card 3.4.
  • 3. Actions Over Talk: The Readiness Check scores concrete commitments (funds shown, site found, branch visited), never verbal enthusiasm.
