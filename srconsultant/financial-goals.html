The Standard of Truth

The Source of Every Number

The Financial Goals workbook is created after owner interviews and before the Blueprint. It is the sole numerical source of truth.

The Iron Rule of Numbers:
"No number goes into the workbook without a source, and no workbook figure goes to the client without the Sr being able to explain where it came from."

The Universal Cell Palette

Every tab adheres strictly to standard financial modeling color coding:

Blue Text: Input
Black Text: Formula
Green Text: Tab Link
Yellow Cell: Estimate

A workbook with yellow cells is a draft. The clarification round exists to convert every yellow estimate to blue input.

Executive Diagnostics

The 8 Critical Red Flags

A red flag is never a reason to bury a number; it is an immediate Radical Candor conversation with the owner. Click any card to inspect.

● Amber Flag

Gross Margin (Food)

Target: 50% to 55%. Check supplier raw item contracts.

● Critical Red

Payback from Signing

Never raise targets to fix this; adjust package or site tier.

● Critical Red

Payback vs. Lease

Capital must be recouped before lease renewal renegotiation.

● Amber Flag

Fees at Break-Even

Check whether fixed fees crush struggling stores.

● Amber Flag

Mall Occupancy Cost

Ceiling is 12% of gross sales for high-traffic mall formats.

● Green (Verified)

Recomputed Costings

Never trust typed numbers; recompute ingredients via formula.

Key Takeaways for Module 3

  • 1. Package vs. Total Cash: The franchise package is never the total cash required to open; working capital (3 months fixed costs) and site fit-out must be budgeted.
  • 2. Payback from Signing: Steady-state payback ignores construction and ramp-up. Always quote payback from signing day.
  • 3. Tax Switch at ₱3M: Models must programmatically toggle from 3% percentage tax to VAT above ₱3,000,000 annual sales.
