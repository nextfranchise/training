Operating Principles

The 6 Ground Rules

Read aloud in Session 1 and held visible across every engagement. Tap or hover any card to flip and view the full rule.

1. Customer First

Where every engagement begins.

↻ Tap to view rule

Customer First

We start from the end customer's and franchisee's life, never from the client's deck, menu, or revenue goals.

Ground Rule #1

2. Their Words, Not Ours

Exact client phrasing.

↻ Tap to view rule

Their Words, Not Ours

Keep the customer's and owner's exact words, Taglish included. A section in our consulting vocabulary is one the owner cannot defend to franchisees.

Ground Rule #2

3. No Invented Facts

Labeling evidence rigorously.

↻ Tap to view rule

No Invented Facts

A fact we did not see or hear directly is marked Inferred until confirmed. A claim from the owner remains Client-said until verified.

Ground Rule #3

4. Say What's Weak, With Care

Radical Candor discipline.

↻ Tap to view rule

Say What's Weak

Criticism of the work is never criticism of the person. Everyone's work is reviewed, including the lead Sr's. Candor starts with leadership.

Ground Rule #4

5. Ends in Action

Proof, owner, date.

↻ Tap to view rule

Ends in Action

Never leave with "Next steps: TBD". Every single session closes with one concrete next proof, one named owner, and one definite date.

Ground Rule #5

6. Info Stays in the Room

Absolute confidentiality.

↻ Tap to view rule

Confidentiality

Client information stays in the room. Client numbers belong to those clients and are never disclosed or reused for another brand.

Ground Rule #6
Practical Baseline

Week 0 Trainee Deliverables

Sent 3 days before Session 1 so month-long progress is measured with tangible evidence.

Item 1: One Stuck Problem

Written in two or three sentences. What client challenge are you currently unable to solve?

Example: "The client wants 20 units in year one, but the main branch took 14 months to break even and supply deliveries arrive 3 days late."

Item 2: Least-Sure Section

Your most recent Blueprint draft section that feels weak or unconvincing.

Example: "Card 1.3 Territory: I used the owner's rule of 50,000 population without calculating penetration or checking actual daily volume."

Item 3: Best Advisor Note

Two or three sentences on the best consultant or advisor you ever worked with.

What they did differently: Almost every behavior listed traces directly to one of the 13 Meta Skills.

Key Takeaways for Module 0

  • 1. Teach, Show, Practice: A definition is never taught alone; every concept is applied directly to a franchise case and a live client.
  • 2. Extended Sessions: Sessions 3, 4, and 5 run 4 hours each because financial models and Blueprint cards are built, not just discussed.
  • 3. Action at the Close: Every session ends with one next proof, one owner, and one date. "Next steps: TBD" is strictly prohibited.
