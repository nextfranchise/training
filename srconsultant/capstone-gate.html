Appendix C Standard

The Four Rubric Proficiency Levels

Every score requires documented behavioral evidence: an artifact, a direct quote, or a verified client outcome. No evidence, no score.

Level 1
Unaware

Does not show the habit; cannot identify when it is missing from a draft section.

Level 2
Aware

Spots it missing in peers' work; inconsistent in applying it to own deliverables.

Level 3
Practicing

Applies it when prompted; deliverables reliably pass the Meta Skills check.

Level 4 · Sr Target
Embodied

Applies it unprompted; coaches others; catches omissions in client framing.
