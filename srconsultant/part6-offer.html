Card 6.2 Framework

The Value Equation in Franchising

Price objections are almost always value objections. Elevate value through four operational levers without slashing fees:

↑ Dream Outcome

Dream Outcome

A proven business in their hometown that allows returning OFWs to stay together.

↑ Perceived Likelihood

Perceived Likelihood

'It already works in a town like mine' (Catubig and Laoang ₱726k–₱862k proof).

↓ Time Delay

Time Delay

Open in 60 days. Snaxxville HQ manages kitchen equipment fabrication and initial stock.

↓ Effort & Sacrifice

Effort & Sacrifice

Snaxxville provides on-site opening trainers and turnkey POS setup.

Quality Audit Clinic

Template B8: Live Offer Audit

Audit the five core offer items. Guarantees must cover only what franchisors control; perks must have calculated costs.

Offer Element Sample Claim Test Criteria Verdict
Guarantee 'Certification Guarantee: trainer stays at our expense until crew certifies.' Does franchisor control it? Yes. (Never guarantee sales or income). Pass
Guarantee 'Guaranteed 100% ROI in 12 months or money back.' Franchisor cannot control local market sales or store management. Revise (Illegal)
Scarcity 'Only 11 territories qualify in Northern Samar; max 2 store openings/mo.' Is it true today? Yes. Grounded in certified trainer capacity. Pass
Urgency 'Limited time: price rises in 5 days!' (Timer resets monthly). False urgency destroys credibility. Revise (Fake)
Perk Costing 'Waived renewal fee (₱118,750 value at Month 61).' Valued at ₱118k, but does zero for 13-month payback. Disclose both. Pass

Key Takeaways for Module 10

  • 1. Guarantees Cover Franchisor Actions: Never guarantee sales, income, payback, or clinical/educational results. Guarantee operational support and crew certification.
  • 2. Perk Value vs. Payback: A perk that takes effect in Year 5 has real financial value but has zero effect on the initial 13-month payback. Disclose both honestly.
  • 3. Signed Side Letters: All founding offer terms, perks, and scarcity commitments must be incorporated into a side letter reviewed by Legal Counsel.
