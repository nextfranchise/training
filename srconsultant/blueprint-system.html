Cascade Effect: An unverified persona (e.g. assuming middle-class mall shoppers instead of local commuters) causes the territory rule to count the wrong catchment, which skews Conservative volume, renders break-even math invalid, and generates false guarantee liabilities in the Offer.

Key Takeaways for Module 4

  • 1. Internal Single Source: Franchisees never see the Blueprint. It is the architectural engine that generates pitch decks, operations manuals, and franchise agreements.
  • 2. Research Never Overwrites the Workbook: If market research challenges a number, bring it back to the client, update the workbook, and then re-align the Blueprint.
  • 3. The Trace Test: Any number on a marketing slide that cannot be traced to a Blueprint card and workbook cell is deemed invalid.
