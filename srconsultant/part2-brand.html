The Turnaround Lesson

Franchisees buy the founder's proven judgment along with the operating manual. The hardest moment usually explains a core rule in the franchise agreement.

The Snaxxville Laoang Case

Two years ago, sales at Snaxxville’s Laoang branch collapsed to single digits. Instead of shutting down, the founders relocated to a prominent corner, added dine-in seating, and improved combo items. Today it generates 116 transactions a day (₱726,268/mo).

The Franchise Rule It Explains: The right site and store format matter more than town population. This turnaround is the direct reason Card 3.7 mandates physical site evaluation before signing.

Key Takeaways for Module 6

  • 1. The Founder's Hardest Part: Always document the hardest year and turnaround. It provides authentic conviction and explains operational rules.
  • 2. Show the Weak Units: Including struggling units demonstrates intellectual honesty and protects future franchisees from selecting bad locations.
  • 3. Franchise-Equivalent P&L: Company-owned P&Ls must be restated with royalties and system fees before being compared to franchise projections.
