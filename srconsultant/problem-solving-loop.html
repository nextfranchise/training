Key Takeaways for Module 11

  • 1. Strict Time Discipline: The Loop runs in 60 minutes flat. Over-debating at Step 2 or 3 usually indicates a lack of primary field data.
  • 2. One Link Only: Trying to fix five links simultaneously dilutes focus and exhausts the client. Attack the single primary driver.
  • 3. Problem Card to Blueprint: Accepted Problem Cards directly populate Card 5.1 Foundation Upgrades or Card 5.2 Risks and Mitigations.
