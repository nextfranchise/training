Facilitation Framework

The Three Moves of Candor

Never present bad news without a constructive next step. Radical Candor maintains high challenge alongside deep personal care.

Move 1

Name the Weak Point

Say it plainly without diplomatic fluff or softening it into nothing.

Move 2

Say Why It Matters

Focus on the franchisee or customer's life, backed by primary evidence.

Move 3

Offer the Next Step

A tangible, respectful way forward that the owner can actually say yes to.

Facilitator Rule #3: Name a Meta Skill to an owner only after the owner has used it organically. Never lecture clients on consulting terminology.
Interactive Simulation

Role-Play 1: The Territory That Isn't Ready

The owner wants to sell a franchise in a 15,000-catchment town to repay a loan, but the territory demand score is 5/10. Choose your response:

Owner: "Look, I need this franchise fee right now because my bank loan is due next month. My food is delicious; it can beat any demographic rule. If we don't sell this town, someone else will open there!"

Key Takeaways for Module 12

  • 1. Candor is Not Harshness: Harshness attacks the person; candor evaluates the work with deep personal respect for the owner.
  • 2. Keep the Buyer in the Room: When owners push to sell to unqualified prospects, remind them of the family savings at risk.
  • 3. Respectful 'Not Yet': Turn down unfit candidates with clear, constructive criteria rather than a flat, insulting refusal.
